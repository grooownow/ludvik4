# Ideas to keep (immersive site brainstorm, 2026-10-22)

- **"hello" lettering.** Owner liked how the word was set: Fredoka 700, huge,
  `#ff4fb6`, tight letter-spacing (-0.02em). Rejected as a first screen
  (mascot covered the text, empty background), but keep the lettering for
  somewhere else. Mockup: `threshold-mockups.html`, option 2 ("Слово-стена").
- **Three style directions** (Poster / Relax / Golden middle) are kept for the
  screen after the intro: `style-directions.html`.
- **First screen = a stage.** No words, at most a small quiet studio name and a
  half-transparent arrow that becomes visible on hover. The mascot is the
  centre, playable. Metaphor over literal copy ("knock knock", "come in" were
  rejected as too literal).

- **Stage chosen: studio + beam** (`../stage-lab.html`). Seamless saturated
  pink cyclorama, soft beam from front-upper-left (between mascot and viewer,
  so the belly reads as volume), glossy highlights, soft shadow. Mascot has no
  mouth here; the mood switch comes back later. Drag left/right spins the
  mascot, drag down/up tilts it toward/away while held; camera and light stay
  fixed. Rejected: draggable lamp, glowing floor plate, "space" scene, jelly
  bubbles, glossy product table. Owner: "for a first screen this is still too
  little" — next step is what else lives on this screen.
- **Exhibit label** (kept, static, right of the head): "EXHIBIT Nº 04 / Ludvik / Pure code, WebGL, zero polygons. / Handle with curiosity." Museum-label format: the medium line names the real tech. Owner loves it.

Renders used in the mockups: `neva-smile.png`, `neva-calm.png`,
`neva-surprised.png` (transparent background, from `../nevalyashka-final.html`).
