# Ludvik4 International Product Canon

This is the internal source of truth for the English-language market. Public
copy, service pages, profiles, proposals, and future articles should use the
same definitions and boundaries.

## Canonical definition

Plain-language definition:

> Ludvik4 is a Europe-based, founder-led web product studio that designs and
> builds focused websites, controlled business workflow automations, MVPs, and
> custom web applications for clients worldwide.

Strategic definition:

> Ludvik4 turns an unclear business problem or product idea into a scoped,
> production-ready digital product with one accountable lead from discovery
> through launch.

`Founder-led web product studio` is the stable category. `Freelance developer`
may describe the operating model in a marketplace profile, but it is not the
site's central category. `Agency` may appear in comparisons; Ludvik4 does not
claim a permanent team.

## Audience

Primary segments:

1. A founder who has a product idea but no credible first-release boundary.
2. A small business whose offer needs a clearer owned website and conversion
   path.
3. A small operations team repeating a process that generic integrations do
   not handle safely.
4. A product owner who needs a focused web tool, customer portal, or existing
   product improvement without hiring a full internal team.

The best client arrives with a problem and a desired outcome, not a complete
technical specification. They value direct communication, explicit scope,
inspectable work, and ownership of the delivered source code.

Not a fit by default:

- native iOS or Android applications;
- staff augmentation sold by the hour without an owned outcome;
- large enterprise transformations requiring a permanent multi-team vendor;
- unmonitored automations where errors can affect money, rights, or customers;
- requests to reproduce a competitor without product discovery or original
  value.

## Service ontology

### Website design and development

Central entity: a responsive, production-ready website built around one
audience, one offer, and one primary conversion path.

Includes discovery, information structure, collaborative copy development,
visual direction, responsive implementation, technical SEO foundations,
analytics setup when legally enabled, testing, launch, and handover.

### Business workflow automation

Central entity: a controlled workflow with defined input and outcome, logs,
validation, error handling, and human approval where risk requires it.

Tools can include scripts, APIs, integrations, bots, and AI. The tool is not the
offer; the reliable process is.

### MVP and custom web app development

Central entity: a production-ready first release around one important problem
and one complete user journey.

Includes first-release scoping, product and data design, UX/UI, frontend,
backend, database, appropriate integrations, automated tests, deployment,
stabilisation, and technical handover.

## Differentiators

1. **One accountable lead.** Product and engineering responsibility do not
   disappear into an account layer.
2. **Focused first release.** Scope is organised around a complete outcome,
   not the largest affordable feature list.
3. **Controlled automation.** Logs, validation, error paths, and human review
   are product requirements, not later polish.
4. **AI with engineering controls.** AI assists delivery inside written
   requirements, version control, automated tests, review, and quality gates.
5. **Handover without lock-in.** Source code, project access, and operating
   documentation belong in the delivery path.

## Evidence policy

Only publish a case when a reader can inspect a public product, repository,
approved screenshot, or client-approved outcome. Do not invent revenue,
conversion, time-saved, delivery-speed, or client-count metrics.

The first international evidence node is `qa-pilot`: public repository,
documentation, releases, and an installable Codex plugin. Projects whose only
public evidence exposes a Russian-language market or `.ru` destination do not
appear on the international site.

## Geography and identity

Canonical public geography: `Europe-based · remote · worldwide`.

Spain may appear where a real-world legal or organisational location is useful,
including the privacy notice and Organization structured data. The international
site must not use Russian-language pages, `.ru` canonicals, Russian-market case
links, or cross-market service files as evidence or discovery targets.

The two domains are separate market storefronts, not hreflang translations:
their commercial pages, evidence, contact path, and content priorities differ.

## Voice and claims

- First person singular for delivery and accountability.
- Direct, calm, specific English; no agency plural unless multiple named
  participants are actually involved.
- Say what is delivered before describing tools.
- Use `AI-assisted`, never `AI-powered` as an empty quality claim.
- Do not promise rankings, guaranteed business outcomes, or universal delivery
  times.
- Do not publish public price anchors until the international pricing model is
  explicitly approved.
